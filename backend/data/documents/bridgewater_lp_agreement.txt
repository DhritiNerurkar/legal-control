CERTIFICATE OF LIMITED PARTNERSHIP
BRIDGEWATER ASSOCIATES LP

State of Delaware
Division of Corporations

Entity Name: Bridgewater Associates, L.P.
Formation Date: January 1, 1975
Entity Type: Limited Partnership

GENERAL PARTNER AUTHORITY:
The General Partner, Bridgewater Associates, Inc., has full power and authority to:
- Manage and control the business and affairs of the Partnership
- Enter into swap agreements, derivative transactions, and financial contracts
- Execute ISDA Master Agreements and related documentation
- Engage in prime brokerage relationships
- Execute repurchase agreements and securities lending transactions

INVESTMENT ACTIVITIES:
The Partnership is authorized to engage in:
- Global macro investment strategies
- Currency, interest rate, equity, and commodity derivatives trading
- Fixed income and equity securities transactions
- Alternative investment strategies

REGULATORY COMPLIANCE:
The Partnership operates as a private fund under Section 3(c)(1) of the Investment Company Act of 1940 and is managed by an SEC-registered investment adviser.

CAPACITY LIMITATIONS:
None, subject to compliance with applicable securities laws and regulations.

NETTING AND SET-OFF:
The Partnership agreement specifically authorizes netting and set-off arrangements in derivative transactions.
